# Lost Chords — Support

Need help or found a problem? Email <lostdevinmyworld@gmail.com>.

It helps to include:

- Your iPhone model and iOS version.
- The song name, and the bar number if the problem is in one place.
- What you expected and what happened instead.

## Common questions

**I bought a plan but the app still shows the free version.**
Open About in the app and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

**How do I cancel a subscription?**
Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

**Which files can I open?**
MIDI (.mid, .midi) and MusicXML (.musicxml, .xml, .mxl) piano files.

**How do I see the intro again?**
Open About in the app and tap See intro.
