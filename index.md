# Lost Chords — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Lost Chords does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data.

## What stays on your device

- **Your songs.** MIDI and MusicXML files you open are copied into the app on your device. They are never uploaded.
- **Your settings.** View, speed, sound and practice choices are saved on your device.
- **Free song check.** To remember which song is your free song, the app saves a short value in this device's Keychain. It never leaves the device and is not linked to you.

## What the app does not do

- No account, no sign-in.
- No analytics, no ads, no tracking.
- No internet connection is used for playing, learning or analysing songs.

## Purchases

Subscriptions and the Lifetime purchase are handled by Apple through the App Store. Lost Chords never sees your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to those payments: <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>.

## Piano connection

When you connect a piano by USB or Bluetooth, the notes you play are read by the app on your device to light up keys and follow you in Play with me. They are not recorded or sent anywhere.

## Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

## Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at <https://github.com/lostinmyworld/lost-chords-privacy/issues>.
