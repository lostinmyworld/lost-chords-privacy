# Lost Chords — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Lost Chords does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data.

## What stays on your device

- **Your songs.** MIDI and MusicXML files you open are copied into the app on your device. They are never uploaded.
- **Your settings.** View, speed, sound and practice choices are saved on your device.
- **Free song and free tries.** To remember which song is your free song and which files you have already tried, the app saves short fingerprints in this device's Keychain. A fingerprint is a code made from a file, not the file itself. It never leaves the device and is not linked to you.

## What the app does not do

- No account, no sign-in.
- No analytics, no ads, no tracking.
- No internet connection is used for playing, learning or analysing songs.

## Purchases

Subscriptions and the Lifetime purchase are handled by Apple through the App Store. Lost Chords never sees your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to those payments: <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>.

## Piano connection

When you connect a piano by USB or Bluetooth, the notes you play are read by the app on your device to light up keys and follow you in Play with me. They are not recorded or sent anywhere. iOS asks for Bluetooth permission only so the app can find your piano.

## Crash reports

If you turn on Share With App Developers in your iPhone's Settings (Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us. They help fix bugs and do not identify you. You can turn this off at any time in the same place.

## Deleting your data

Everything stays on your device, so you are in control:

- Delete a song in the app to remove it.
- Delete the app to remove all songs and settings.
- The free song and free tries fingerprints may stay in this device's Keychain after the app is deleted. They hold no personal information.

We hold no copy of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side. You can still ask us anything about it using the contact below.

## Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.
